## Privacy Policy

I am Zoran Stankovic. You can contact me about this website and the processing of personal data at blog.zs@proton.me or at the postal address above.

### Hosting

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. When you visit the site, your browser sends technical information, such as your IP address and the requested page, to Cloudflare so the site can be delivered to you. Cloudflare may also process this information to keep its services secure. The legal basis for processing this information to deliver and secure the website is my legitimate interest in operating a reliable website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). I do not use website analytics. Cloudflare retains technical data in accordance with its service and security requirements; I do not maintain a separate database of site visitors. Cloudflare may process data outside the European Economic Area; information about its transfer safeguards is available in its [Data Processing Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/). For more information, see [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

### Links to YouTube videos

Some blog posts link to videos on YouTube. The preview images are stored on this website, so reading a post does not connect your browser to YouTube. Only if you follow a video link does YouTube's website open in a new tab; from then on, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that visit. For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Theme preference

If you choose a light or dark theme, this website saves your choice in your browser's local storage so it can remember your preference on future visits. Storing and reading this setting is necessary to provide the theme preference you requested (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). The website does not transmit this preference to me or third parties. The preference remains in your browser until you clear your browser's local storage.

### Contact by email

If you contact me by email, I use the information you send to read and respond to your message. Messages sent to my published address are handled by [Proton Mail](https://proton.me/legal/privacy). The legal basis is my legitimate interest in responding to your enquiry (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). I delete messages when they are no longer needed for this purpose, unless I need to keep them longer for legal reasons. Unsolicited spam is deleted without reply.

### Your rights

If I process your personal data, you have the right, under the applicable conditions, to request access to, correction or deletion of your data, to restrict its processing, and to receive a copy in a portable format where applicable. You can contact me at blog.zs@proton.me. If I cannot identify you from the information available to me, I may need further information to handle your request. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, including the [data protection authority of Saxony-Anhalt](https://datenschutz.sachsen-anhalt.de/).

### Right to object

Where I process your personal data based on my legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. If you object, I will stop processing your data unless I can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for continuing or need the data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. You can object by emailing blog.zs@proton.me.
